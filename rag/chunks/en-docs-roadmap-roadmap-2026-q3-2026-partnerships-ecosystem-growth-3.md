# RoadMap 2026

Executing our vision for a decentralized, agent-driven economy.

## Q3 2026 - Partnerships & Ecosystem Growth

- **White-Label & Reseller Program:** Enable partners to resell Verifik services with competitive margins, white-label API & UI, co-branding options, and partner dashboard.
- **Professional Services:** Implementation and consulting services including custom integration development, compliance consulting, training & onboarding, and custom feature development.
- **Data Intelligence & Insights Platform:** Transform raw data into actionable business intelligence with market intelligence, predictive analytics, custom dashboards, and automated compliance reports. Tiered pricing structure.
- **Webhook & Event Streaming Premium:** Advanced webhook features including custom event filtering, retry logic, event batching, webhook analytics, and real-time event streaming with WebSocket connections.
- **User Rewards:** USDC/Token incentives for users validating their own data.
- **Integrate inside Zelf Name Service:** Consumer app for managing identity assets and rewards.
- **DeFi Partnerships:** Integration with lending protocols for reputation-based access.
- **Data Marketplace:** Protocol for users to monetize specific data points privately.

## Q4 2026 - Advanced Tools & Global Expansion

- **Unified Compliance Dashboard:** Single platform integrating KYC, KYB, AML screening, and transaction monitoring. Comprehensive case management system with risk overview dashboard for compliance teams.
- **Reusable KYC / KYC Sharing:** Enable verified identity data sharing between platforms with user consent. Reduces redundant verification steps, accelerates onboarding, and improves user experience while maintaining full compliance.
- **No-Code Workflow Builder:** Visual workflow designer enabling businesses to customize verification paths, set escalation rules, and automate reviews based on dynamic conditions without developer input.
- **Adverse Media Analysis:** Monitor thousands of media sources for negative mentions and risk indicators. Automated categorization and risk assessment with real-time alerts for compliance teams.
- **Transaction Monitoring:** Pattern detection and suspicious activity monitoring for AML compliance. Real-time alerts and automated reporting for financial services and regulated industries.
- **Asian Market Research & Planning:** Market research, partner identification, and initial pilot programs for Asian markets (India, Southeast Asia, Japan & South Korea). Foundation for future expansion.
- **DAO Governance:** Community voting on new registry integrations and protocol parameters.
- **European Expansion:** Initial pilots for eIDAS compatibility.
- **AI Fraud Layer:** Decentralized machine learning nodes for anomaly detection.
- **Cross-Chain Bridge:** Interoperability with other major L1 networks.
