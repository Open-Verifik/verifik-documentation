# Identity Verification API for Government Database Validation | Verifik

**Fast, secure and automated verification**
Properly verifying official documents is key to complying with KYC (Know your customer) / AML (anti-money laundering) regulations, preventing fraud, and building trust in digital transactions.
With Verifik, you can validate official documents in seconds by cross-checking the information captured from the document against local and international government sources.
In the digital age, this data is essential for:
- User authentication.
- Digital onboarding.
- Fraud prevention.
- Regulatory compliance.
- Electronic signatures and contracts.
- Access to financial services, insurance, education, telecommunications, and more.

## Identity Document Guide

Different countries use different citizen identification systems with their respective terms. Verifik unifies these under a single search parameter.

**Identifiers by country:**

- [**CI** (Cédula de Identidad) or Identity Card:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-latam/) Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Nicaragua, Uruguay, Venezuela.
- [**DNI** (Documento Nacional de Identidad) or National Identity Document:](https://verifik.co/en/dni-verification-latin-america/) Argentina, Spain, Honduras, Peru.
- [**CC** (Cédula de Ciudadanía) or Citizen Card:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-colombia/) Colombia.
- [**CURP** (Clave Única de Registro de Población) or Unique Population Registry Code:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-curp-mexico/) Mexico.
- [**DPI** (Documento Personal de Identificación) or Personal Identification Document:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-dpi-guatemala/) Guatemala.
- [**DUI** (Documento Único de Identidad) or Unique Identity Document:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-dui-el-salvador/) El Salvador.
- [**CIE** (Cédula de Identidad y Electoral) or Identity and Electoral Card:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-cie-dominican-republic/) Dominican Republic.
- [**CIC** (Cédula de Identidad Civil) or Civil Identity Card:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-cic-paraguay/) Paraguay.
- [**CID** (Cédula de Identidad Personal) or Personal ID Card:](https://verifik.co/en/identity-verification-cip-panama/) Panama.
- **Carné de Identidad or Identity Card:** Cuba.
