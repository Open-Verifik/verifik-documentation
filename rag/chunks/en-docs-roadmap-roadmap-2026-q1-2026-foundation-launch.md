# RoadMap 2026

## Q1 2026 - Foundation & Launch

Executing our vision for a decentralized, agent-driven economy.

## Q1 2026 - Foundation & Launch

- **Mainnet Launch:** Deployment on Avalanche C-Chain.
- **x402 Protocol Implementation:** Enable autonomous agents to make micro payments on-demand using HTTP 402 Payment Required. Agents can negotiate and pay for data access instantly without credit cards or login forms.
- **$ZNS Token Integration:** Staking mechanism for cost reduction (0.20 → 0.02 credits) and payment processing with benefits.
- **Agent Micro Payment System:** All agent-to-agent and agent-to-service payments utilize $ZNS token with staking benefits, enabling seamless micro transactions for autonomous traffic.
- **Smart Agent Chat:** Multiple concurrent request handling.
- **KYB Flows for SmartEnroll:** Business verification flows now available after successful KYC completion in SmartEnroll. Launched as premium add-on feature.
- **SmartVehicle Premium Launch:** Advanced monitoring tool with configurable settings to track vehicle endpoints at custom frequencies. Monitors SOAT expiration, new infractions, owner changes, and other critical events. Essential for use cases like banks providing loans to car dealers, enabling real-time notifications when vehicle ownership changes. Launched as premium SaaS product with tiered pricing.
- **SmartDriver Premium Launch:** Continuous driver license monitoring service for ride-sharing, delivery, logistics, and HR departments. Tracks license expiration, suspensions, violations, and compliance. Tiered pricing structure.
- **Analytics Premium:** Enhanced data export and analytics features with premium reporting, custom dashboards, and data visualization.
- **Data Tools:** PDF Export via Webhooks & Bulk processing (Excel/CSV/JSON).
- **Expansion:** 5+ new validation endpoints for LATAM.
