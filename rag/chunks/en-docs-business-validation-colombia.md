# Colombia Business Validation

## Available Services

Business validation services for Colombia, including DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) verification and legal invoicer verification for Colombian companies.

## Available Services

- **[Colombia DIAN Verification](/business-validation/colombia-dian-verification)** - Validate Colombian companies' DIAN status and tax authority data
- **[Colombia Legal Invoicer Verification](/business-validation/colombia-legal-invoicer-verification)** - Validate legal invoicer registration in DIAN for Colombian companies

## Features

- **DIAN Verification**: Verify company registration and tax status with DIAN
- **Legal Invoicer Verification**: Validate legal invoicer registration for companies
- **Real-time Data**: Access current and up-to-date information from official DIAN sources
- **Company Validation**: Verify company information, registration status, and compliance

For detailed API documentation and integration guides, please refer to the specific service documentation.
