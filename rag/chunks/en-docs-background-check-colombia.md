# Colombia Background Check

## Available Services

Comprehensive background check services for Colombia, including police record checks, disciplinary records verification, compliance checks, and other criminal history verification services.

## Available Services

- **[Verify Police Compliance with Corrective Measures](/background-check/colombia/verify-police-compliance-with-corrective-measures)** - Check compliance with corrective measures
- **[Colombia Disciplinary Records (Attorney's Office)](/background-check/colombia-disciplinary-records-attorneys-office)** - Check disciplinary records from the Attorney General's Office
- **[Colombia INPEC](/background-check/colombia-inpec)** - Check prison records
- **[Colombia Comptroller Certificate](/background-check/colombia-comptroller-certificate)** - Verify comptroller records
- **[Colombia Public Contracts](/background-check/colombia-public-contracts)** - Verify public contract records

## Features

- **Police Records**: Verify criminal history and pending legal matters with Colombian judicial authorities
- **Disciplinary Records**: Check disciplinary records from the Attorney General's Office
- **Compliance Verification**: Verify compliance with corrective measures
- **Prison Records**: Access INPEC records for prison history
- **Public Records**: Verify public contracts and comptroller records
- **Real-time Data**: Access current and up-to-date information from official sources

For detailed API documentation and integration guides, please refer to the specific service documentation.
