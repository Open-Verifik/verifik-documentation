# U.S. Consolidated Screening List (CSL)

The **Consolidated Screening List (CSL)** is maintained by the **U.S. International Trade Administration (ITA)** in collaboration with the Departments of Commerce, State, and the Treasury. It consolidates **12 distinct export screening and trade restriction lists** into a single federal clearinghouse.

Conducting business with parties listed on the CSL may violate U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR), International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR), or trade sanction regimes, resulting in severe civil and criminal penalties, seizure of shipments, and forfeiture of U.S. export privileges.

---

## The 12 Consolidated Federal Lists

Verifik's CSL API screens all 12 official lists concurrently:

| Agency | List Name | Acronym | Regulatory Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DOC (BIS)** | **Entity List** | `EL` | Parties posing risks of diverting items to weapons of mass destruction or military end-use. |
| **DOC (BIS)** | **Denied Persons List** | `DPL` | Individuals and entities denied U.S. export privileges. |
| **DOC (BIS)** | **Unverified List** | `UVL` | Parties where BIS could not complete end-use check authentications. |
| **DOC (BIS)** | **Military End User List** | `MEU` | Military end-users subject to specialized export license requirements. |
| **DOS (DDTC)** | **ITAR Debarred** | `DTC` | Entities disqualified from participating in defense article and services exports. |
| **DOS (ISN)** | **Nonproliferation Sanctions** | `ISN` | Sanctioned for chemical, biological, or missile nonproliferation violations. |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **Sectoral Sanctions Identifications** | `SSI` | Targets operating in specific Russian economy sectors (energy, defense, finance). |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **Foreign Sanctions Evaders** | `FSE` | Non-U.S. persons facilitating sanctions evasion on Syria and Iran. |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **Non-SDN CMIC** | `CMIC` | Chinese Military-Industrial Complex defense and surveillance technology firms. |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **Non-SDN Palestinian Legislative Council** | `PLC` | Banned Palestinian Legislative Council members elected on the Hamas slate. |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **CAPTA List** | `CAPTA` | Foreign financial institutions subject to correspondent account restrictions. |
| **Treasury (OFAC)** | **Non-SDN Menu-Based Sanctions** | `NS-MBS` | Specific sanctions prohibitions governed by Executive Orders. |

## API Reference

### Endpoint

```http
GET https://api.verifik.co/v2/csl
```

### Headers

| Name          | Value              |
| ------------- | ------------------ |
| Content-Type  | `application/json` |
| Accept        | `application/json` |
| Authorization | `Bearer <token>`   |

### Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `fullName` | string | **Conditional** | Complete corporate entity name or individual name (e.g. `HUAWEI TECHNOLOGIES`). |
| `documentNumber` | string | **Conditional** | Tax ID, national registry number, or passport number. |
| `documentType` | string | No | Document type (e.g., `NIT`, `PAS`, `EIN`). |

### Use Cases

- **Global Trade & Supply Chain Compliance:** Prevent prohibited exports, transfers, and transshipments of hardware, software, and technology.
- **Contractor & Vendor Screening:** Ensure international suppliers and distributors are clear of BIS or ITAR debarment orders.
- **Cross-Border M&A:** Verify target acquisition entities have not committed EAR export control violations.
